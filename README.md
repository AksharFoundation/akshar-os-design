# Akshar App — Design

This repository holds the **executive summary** and **technical designs** for the Akshar App.

Akshar is a super app for education operations. One product, several ways in: people use it as themselves, as a school team, or as an organisation.

## Who it is for

| View | Who | What they see |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Individual** | Student, teacher, parent | Personal work, learning, and communication |
| **Team** | Local school (GSP) or Head Office (HO) | Shared school or HO operations |
| **Organisation** | The organisation as a whole | Cross-school / network-level view |

## What’s in this repo

- **Executive summary** — product intent, audience, and the role model above
  - [Why the Akshar App](executive-summary/why-the-akshar-app.md) — why an app is needed at Akshar today
  - [Flyer](executive-summary/why-the-akshar-app-flyer.html) — one-page A4 poster
- **Technical designs** — architecture, data, APIs, and how the three views share one platform

Designs live here as documents, not application code.
